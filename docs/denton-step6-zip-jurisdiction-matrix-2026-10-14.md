Read-only, geography-based (no radius logic). Generated by `scripts/denton-step6-matrix.py` + `scripts/denton-step6-build.py` (runner, task `step6-compute`);
machine-readable twin: `data/denton-step6-zip-jurisdiction-matrix.json`. Reuses the evidence in `docs/denton-data-integrity-launch-gate-2026-10-10.md` and the registry.

## Method and limits

- ZIP polygon = geo.zip_membership_boundary (canonical); city limits = Denton County GIS City_Limit/0 (+ Census TIGERweb incorporated places for parts outside the county layer); ETJ = Denton County GIS ETJ_GC/1; area shares are geodesic ST_Area ratios; no radius logic. SRID 4269/4326 differences (~1 m) ignored.
- ETJ outside Denton County is UNKNOWN (county layer does not cover it); ZIPs extending into Dallas/Collin/Tarrant/Cooke counties show that share as city or 'outside county'.
- Census place boundaries lag annexations; the county layer's last-edit dates are 2025-2026.
- Stored-record attribution is ZIP-level (serving membership), not jurisdiction-level: a stored record is not geolocated to a city here.
- Texas permit-authority text is a statutory generalization, not verified per jurisdiction.
- PO Box ZIPs 75027, 75029, 76202, 76204, 76206 are out of scope (no geographic area).

**Permit-authority caveat.** General Texas framework, NOT verified against any city's ordinances, interlocal agreements or the county's rules: municipalities regulate (zoning, building permits) inside their limits; a city's subdivision/plat authority also reaches its ETJ (Local Gov't Code ch. 212); counties have no zoning power and only limited authority (plat approval in unincorporated areas under ch. 232, plus floodplain / on-site sewage / 911 addressing programs). Which office actually issues a given permit in a given spot must be confirmed with that jurisdiction.
