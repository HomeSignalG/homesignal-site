Overview

A six-story building with 98 apartments and 1,932 square feet of retail, at the corner of N 26th Street and N Madison Street in Tacoma.

It replaces a vacant former bank building. The City of Tacoma found no significant environmental impacts, on one condition: soil testing before demolition.

City of Tacoma MDNS, LU25-0180

Latest verified change

Sep 9, 2026 · Filed · City of Tacoma

Shoring permits filed with the City of Tacoma

A site development permit (SDEV26-0272) and a building permit for retaining walls (BLDCA26-0284). Tacoma's permit system shows the retaining-wall permit as Ready to Issue and the site permit as needing a pre-construction meeting.

Does not change the project's lifecycle. These permits cover shoring, not the building.

Open Tacoma permit BLDCA26-0284 · See full timeline

Statuses as read from Tacoma's permit data on Oct 29, 2026.

What we know

  • The city issued a Mitigated Determination of Non-Significance (LU25-0180) on Aug 27, 2026. Source
  • The project is a permitted use in its zone and needs no discretionary land use permit. Source
  • The site is inside the former Asarco smelter plume. The city requires soil testing before demolition. Source
  • A demolition permit (DEMOC26-0014) shows Permit Issued in Tacoma's permit system. Source

Not verified yet

  • When the demolition permit was issued. JODY's records show its status, not the date.
  • Who owns the land. It is not stated in the documents reviewed.
  • The site plan, Phase I environmental assessment and soil management plan. They are listed but not read.
  • Whether the city's MDNS calls the site "undeveloped" by mistake; the checklist and traffic study describe a paved former bank.

Why this matters

  • Traffic. The traffic study expects 675 weekday trips, 34 in the morning peak hour and 54 in the evening peak hour, and concludes no significant impact.

    Official record Traffic impact analysis (Heath & Associates) · Source

  • Soil. Arsenic in the 40 to 100 ppm range is mapped for the site, and an old diesel tank was closed in place in 1996.

    Official record City MDNS and environmental checklist · Source

  • Housing. 98 apartments, about 20% designated for low income, and 0.68 parking stalls per unit.

    Official record Environmental checklist and traffic study · Source

What happens next

The comment period on the city's environmental determination closed Sept 10, 2026. The city says appeals go to Pierce County Superior Court within 21 days after the building permit is issued.

Public comment on the environmental determination: closed Sep 10, 2026. Read the decision it concerned

No permit for the main building appears in the Tacoma permit records we track. Last checked Oct 29, 2026.
