Overview

A data center campus proposed for ranch land in Hansel Valley, in western Box Elder County.

In October 2026, Bar H Ranch, Inc. asked the Utah Division of Water Rights to move water now used to irrigate the ranch to the project. The filing names O'Leary Digital Utah DC Sub I, LLC as the developer.

The state has not decided the filing, and it has been protested.

Utah Division of Water Rights: Change Details for a55167

Latest verified change

Oct 15, 2026 · Filed · Utah Division of Water Rights

Change application a55167 filed on water right 13-2

Bar H Ranch, Inc. asks to change where, how and for what the water of right 13-2 is used: up to 1,712 acre-feet a year (4 cubic feet per second) from Salt Wells Spring Stream, moving from ranch irrigation to power generation and data-center uses. The Division lists it as Unapproved and Protested.

Does not change the project's lifecycle. It is an application, not an approval.

What we know

  • Bar H Ranch, Inc. filed change application a55167 on Oct 15, 2026. The Division lists it Unapproved and Protested. Source
  • The filing says the water would supply a data center project to be developed by O'Leary Digital Utah DC Sub I, LLC, which is under contract to buy the water right. Source
  • 82 parcels, about 35,600 acres, held by four companies, surround the filing's diversion points. Source
  • MIDA and the Box Elder County Commission put Stratos items on their Apr 24 and May 4, 2026 agendas. Source

Not verified yet

  • Whether MIDA and the County adopted the items on those agendas. JODY has the agendas, not the minutes.
  • Which company MIDA's development agreement names as master developer.
  • Where buildings would go. The land assembly is not a site plan.
  • The text of the County Attorney's referendum decision. Its outcome so far comes from news reports.
  • Any state air permit. None was found in the sources we track.
  • The water-use and depletion figures, which are the applicant's own analysis.

Why this matters

  • Water. Up to 1,712 acre-feet a year from Salt Wells Spring Stream. The applicant says the project would consume no more than 1,009 acre-feet, against 1,009 to 1,291.6 acre-feet consumed by irrigation today.

    Official record Applicant's statement in the state filing · Source

  • Power. The filing lists a power use: "Wonder Valley Power ... Steam Generation Power Plant, Rated at 7500 MW".

    Official record State filing · Source

  • Air and health. A study reported by Utah News Dispatch and KSL projects health effects from the project's emissions. The developer's team disputes it.

    Reported, not verified Reported by news outlets, not verified by JODY · Source

What happens next

The Division's record shows no hearing and no State Engineer decision on a55167. It is marked Protested.

No upcoming official hearing or deadline has been confirmed in the sources we track. Last checked Oct 28, 2026.
