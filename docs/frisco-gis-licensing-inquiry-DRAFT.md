**To:** City of Frisco GIS (maps.friscotexas.gov) — and, for item 3, Planning Division (ProjectInput@FriscoTexas.gov, 972-292-5300)
**From:** HomeSignal
**Subject:** Use of the public "Proposed Zoning" layer in paid property-intelligence reports

Hello,

HomeSignal publishes property reports that tell a buyer or owner which official development activity sits near an address. We would like to include records from the City's public ArcGIS layer **External_Planning_and_Zoning / Proposed Zoning** (MapServer layer 5), which carries the copyright line "City of Frisco GIS".

We would be grateful for your answers to the following:

1. **Use.** May the zoning-case data (project number, project name, status, application date, link to the eTRAKiT record) and the polygon geometry be included in reports that HomeSignal sells to paying customers? If conditions apply (a licence, terms of use, a fee, or a written permission), please tell us what they are.
2. **Attribution.** If permitted, what attribution would you like? We would credit "City of Frisco" and link each record back to its eTRAKiT page, and we would state the date we last read the data.
3. **Definitions.** To avoid misdescribing a case, we need the City's own meaning of these `Status` values, and of the `Applied_Date` field:
   - `UNDER REVIEW` and `REVISE AND RESUBMIT` — are both active applications with no decision yet?
   - `REVIEW COMPLETE` — does this mean staff review is finished and the case awaits a hearing, or that an outcome exists?
   - `Applied_Date` — is this the original filing date or the most recent resubmittal date?
   - A case named "(Withdrawn as SUP25-0008)" shows `UNDER REVIEW` — is that a re-filed case, and is the earlier number closed?
4. **Cadence and stability.** Is the layer updated on a regular schedule, and are the service URL and field names expected to remain stable?
5. **Personal data.** The layer exposes staff contact fields (planner name and e-mail). We do **not** request or publish them; please confirm you have no objection.

We will not reproduce the data in any way that implies the City endorses HomeSignal or that a listed case has been approved. Thank you for your time.
