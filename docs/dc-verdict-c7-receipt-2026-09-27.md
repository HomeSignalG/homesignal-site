# C7: the withheld Atlas pins re-checked under the shared rule — receipt, 2026-09-27

**Finding: 3 of the Atlas pins withheld from Map 1 rest on a geocode of the WRONG street**, and 5 pins
carry a "corroborated by address" flag earned from the opposite side of the road. The cause is one gap in
the ONE derived-point verdict every source uses. It checked the house number and the state, but not the
street's direction, and it read "31" out of "31st Avenue" as a house number.

**Change (APPLIED 2026-09-27 15:57 UTC, verified after the 16:35 resolver):** `dc_derived_point_verdict` also rejects
(`REJECTED_MATCH_DIVERGES`):
- a match whose leading street direction contradicts the query's;
- a query or match with no house number (digits, one optional letter, then a space).

A direction present on only one side is never a rejection. No source is named and nothing moves a pin.

## 3. How it ships

The same discipline as C3c, adapted for a change that reaches Map 1 through the resolver:

- **Artifacts.** `docs/dc-verdict-apply.sql` and `docs/dc-verdict-rollback.sql` are generated by
  `test/dc_verdict_pg/build_verdict.py` from the DDL of record.
  - The rollback is proven to fingerprint to production's pre-C7 verdict, `4cd5b97d…`.
  - Both artifacts run a drift guard and a behavioural post-condition.
- **Gate.** `scripts/dc-verdict-gate.sh` + `docs/dc-verdict-gate.sql`, 12 checks, run on a replica of
  production:
  - parity with production's Map 1;
  - both resolvers run BEFORE the change (so only C7's own effect is compared), then after it, then
    again (steady state);
  - every flip equals an independently worded prediction;
  - every Map 1 change reads a flipped address and is of an allowed kind (restored / corroboration
    withdrawn / removed-from-a-flipped-derived-point);
  - no pin moved, identity unchanged, every other entity's geography byte-identical.
- **Apply.** `scripts/dc-verdict-apply.sh`, via `dc-verdict-apply.yml` (confirm `APPLY-VERDICT-C7`):
  - it needs the offline proof and the gate to pass in the same run;
  - it refuses the :25/:35 window;
  - the lock wait is bounded;
  - definition parity is checked afterwards.
  - Rollback uses `ROLLBACK-VERDICT-C7`.

**Offline proof** (`test/dc_verdict_pg/offline.sh`), all 7 checks pass locally:

- The gate passes on the real artifact: 2 flips, Dir OSM restored, 2 corroborations withdrawn.
- The gate REFUSES a consistently wrong verdict. That verdict also rejects a directionless query, and its
  artifact's own post-condition passes; the gate's `G03` catches it.
- The apply refuses drift.
- A held lock fails the apply after 6 s.
- The apply succeeds, and after the resolver Map 1 moves `7/2 canon 1/1 dir 0 -> 8/1 canon 1/0 dir 1`.
- A second apply is refused.
- Rollback plus the resolver restores Map 1 byte for byte, and re-apply works.

**Other suites.** Epoch, OSM, Atlas validation, zip membership, Map 1 publication and canonical geography
all pass. The structure test is `test/dc-verdict-structure.test.mjs` (14 checks); three of its pins were
mutation-checked, and all three mutations were caught.
